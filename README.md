**Solmar Bay — Open-World Crime** is a 3D open-world crime game that runs entirely
from static files in your browser (three.js, no build step, no network requests).
You are Rico Marlin, a courier fresh off the bus in Solmar Bay, a sun-soaked coastal
city of five districts — Marina, Old Town, Hills, Port and Downtown. Madame Vela, the
Bay's fixer, has three jobs for you: a sunset courier run, a stolen Sunset GT, and a
police escape. Between jobs, explore the city on foot or behind the wheel, steal cars,
dodge traffic and pedestrians, and stay ahead of the Solmar Bay PD as your wanted
level climbs.

What's in the game: a procedural city with a beach, pier and port; traffic and
pedestrians; cars you can steal and drive; a 5-star wanted system with patrol cars and
officers who chase and arrest you; three chained story missions with cash rewards;
punch and shoot combat; a HUD with cash, health, wanted stars, a live minimap and
mission guidance; a start screen, pause menu and autosave (localStorage); and
procedural WebAudio sound — engine hum, sirens, gunshots and a sunset pad.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome.

Controls: WASD move & drive · mouse drag camera · wheel zoom · E enter / steal car ·
F punch · click shoot · Space handbrake · P pause · M mute.
